		I am an applied AI, robotics, and software engineer with a practical background in building real systems: AI workflows, RAG pipelines, backend services, robotics experiments, simulation environments, web platforms, and mobile products. 		## Current Focus

		### Applied AI & Agentic Systems

		I build systems that combine LLMs, retrieval, structured prompting, tool-calling patterns, and explainable workflows. Recent examples include [TalkingHeadAI](/projects/talking-head-ai/), a real-time conversational mentor platform with retrieval routing, long-term memory, bilingual interaction, and a mentor dashboard; and the [NSP AI Enquiry Workflow](/projects/nsp-ai-enquiry-workflow/), which converts unstructured customer enquiries into structured JSON for operational use.

		I also work on agent-style systems for evaluation and automation, such as [QoE-BugHunter (VizDoom)](/projects/qoe-bughunter-vizdoom/), a multi-agent prototype for discovering, verifying, and reporting reproducible quality-of-experience issues in 3D environments.

		### Robotics, Simulation & Reinforcement Learning

		My robotics work is hands-on and experiment-driven. I built the [Ned3 Pro DRL Sim-to-Real Reaching](/projects/ned3pro-mujoco-rl-bundle/) pipeline using SAC+HER, MuJoCo, ROS, and real-hardware validation. I also developed a [Ned3 Pro Adaptive Controller](/projects/ned3pro-adaptive-controller/) for current-mode actuation when a direct torque interface was not available.

		To support repeatable robotics experimentation, I built a [MuJoCo Docker Bridge for Ned3 Pro](/projects/mujoco-docker-bridge-ned3pro/) and university simulation projects such as [Robotic Navigation in Unity](/projects/robotic-navigation/) and [Agent Based Modelling](/projects/agent-based-disease-modelling-netlogo/).

		### AI/ML Engineering

		I work with Python, PyTorch, FastAPI, vector databases, Docker, and structured evaluation workflows. One example is the [CNN-based Persian Digit Recognizer](/projects/cnn-based-persian-digit-recognizer-pytorch/), a PyTorch image-classification pipeline with training, evaluation, inference, and confusion-matrix reporting.

		### Web, Mobile & Commerce Systems

		Before moving deeper into AI and robotics, I spent years building production web and app systems. My commerce work includes [Anotah Multi-Region E-commerce Website](/projects/anotah-website/), [Anotah Mobile App](/projects/anotah-mobile-app/), [Eleganza Concept Website](/projects/eleganza-concept/), [Nil Home E-commerce Website](/projects/nil-home/), and [Pasha Group Websites](/projects/pasha-group-websites/). These projects shaped how I think about performance, user flow, content operations, SEO, and maintainable product delivery.
